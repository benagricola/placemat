## Routing evidence qualification

The first routing measurement exposed a gap in the historical reference
summary: global clean connectivity can be complete while a phase still has
required-width failures. The retained route reports contain those findings;
the old baseline summary does not. A connectivity improvement alone is not
evidence of a valid board or of unchanged required-width performance.

The reference comparison must retain measured widths, final phase closures
and routing quality, and distinguish missing historical evidence from zero
failures. Preserve the old baseline when those facts cannot be compared.
Correct the measurement record from retained final route reports without
repeating the physical routing runs. Do not describe that correction as a
new routing regression or as proof of no regression.

The corrected reference controls passed 79 tests, with an independent review
finding no remaining blockers. The retained human-placement reports were
augmented without rerouting, binding each board/width to its final report's
path and SHA-256 and preserving its measured copper quality, time, DRC and
source versions. All 16 comparisons are incomparable for the newly required
historical evidence. Their legacy connectivity/DRC comparison is unchanged
for 15 measurements and improved for one; that is not a complete routed
no-regression certificate. Two measurements meet the strengthened reference
pass condition; the others expose open joins, DRC or width/phase failures.
The intentionally obstructed local fixture retained its expected failure
evidence.

The second placement-and-routing board has clean closure 95.29% -> 93.33%,
non-pour opens 9 -> 15, pour opens 3 -> 2, and new manufacturing DRC unchanged
at 6. This is an observed legacy comparison loss, separate from the missing
historical width/phase facts. The step 1 handover already records that board's
blocked later-phase routing as a known limitation. The user chose to ship
steps 1 and 2 for testing on their board instead of further corpus tuning.
Retain this loss explicitly; do not replace the baseline or claim no worse.
