# One Score Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every placement decision (the search's spot, the turn check, explore's ranking, the pin study's weights)
lowers one board objective, the run score in `score.py`, through each term's exact incremental change; the search's
clique wire and leaf-crossing estimate are replaced by the MST change if the gate passes.

**Architecture:** In three phases. **A, the definition:** the run score gains its new terms and pricing (airwire on
signal nets, a declared-link term, crossing prices by net kind, congestion always counted, the fitted frame's area,
escapes judged by surface lanes), lands with the clique search unchanged, and the bench and reference baselines are
re-recorded on it. **B, the incremental forms:** each term gets an incremental form, native first with a Python twin,
behind one `score_delta.Delta` per lifted item; a test holds every term's incremental change equal to the difference
of the whole-board values. **C, the users:** the search (behind a temporary switch for the gate), the turn check, the
pin study and explore move onto it; the gate compares clique and MST on the new score; the step's removal-ledger rows
go; after step 1 merges, test (b) closure decides adoption.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, Rust (pyo3, `native/`), pytest with xdist, `fixtures/bench.py`,
`fixtures/reference/place_ref.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-one-score-design.md`, and
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-surface-escapes-design.md` (written for this step). Also read
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-roadmap-0.100.md` ("Attribution", "Gates", the removal-ledger rows marked 2,
"Keeping dead code out"), `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-06/placement-inventory.md` sections 1 and 3, and
`docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-07-refine-spike.md` ("Native parts"). Every file:line below was read at placemat

## Decisions

Each entry says who decided it. "Alt" is what changes if it is reversed.

Decided by the user on 2026-10-07:
- D1 **Airwire on signal nets.** The run score's airwire is the MST length of the nets that pull: plane and free nets
  (`Board._plane_nets() | Board._free_nets`) leave it, as the search's clique skips them today (layout.py:4873).
  Alt: every net pulls, about 7x the wire work per part on modules (measured on the Mcu module: 4 vs 29 pair
  evaluations per part).
- D2 **A link term.** The run score gains `link`: the sum over declared links whose two pads are placed of the link's
  weight times its pad-to-pad length, at `score.link` (default 1.0). The MST airwire stays unweighted. A link on a
  quiet net pulls only through this term, as `_targets` lets it now (layout.py:4885-4887). Alt: links pull only past
  their limit, and `weight=` loses its meaning (a breaking change).
- D3 **Speed gate by kind.** A part's candidate scores under about 5 us natively; a cell's no slower than today's
  scorer on the same candidate (wire plus crossings 77 us median on the core's cells, refine spike). Measured in Task
  10, with the numbers sent to the user before the gate is fixed. Alt: 5 us for all, which cells fail.
- D4 **`score.area` from a sweep.** Task 6 runs the fitted reference modules at 0.1, 0.25 and 1.0 per mm2; the user
  picks the default from the numbers. Until then the setting's default is 0.25 per mm2, marked provisional in the
  ledger.
- D5 **Crossing prices by net kind, the pin study's values.** `score.crossing_pair_net` 5.0 and
  `score.impedance` 3.0 (D8). A net's kind is the pin study's (`pinmap_input.net_kind`, pinmap_input.py:195-205):
  plane (quiet), pair (a half of a net-class pair, `pairs.board_pairs`, as step 1's D19 keeps for placement),
  impedance (its class names a tuning profile), else plain.
- D6 **Escapes judged by surface lanes**, specced in `2026-10-07-surface-escapes-design.md` and built in this step
  (Tasks 4 and 9). User: fixed 1 mm corridors miss a capacitor 1-2 mm off an IC, and a surface route out is better than
  dropping a via.
- D7 **Gate on the new score.** The new definition lands first with the clique search, the bench and reference
  baselines are re-recorded on it (Task 6), then clique and MST are compared on it (Task 15).

Decided by the user on 2026-10-07, after an outside review relayed by the step 1 session:
- D11 **Legality before closure.** `score.rank` judges legality first: no DRC error beyond the board's baseline, every
  required width met on the final board, no critical finding. Then the phases' closures (judged on the final board,

Decided by the user on 2026-10-07, on the plan:

## Controller rulings

Not user decisions. Each gives its reason and its cost if wrong.

- R1 A crossing's price: `score.crossing_plane` when either net is quiet; `score.pair_crossing / score.crossing` when
  the two are one pair's halves; else the dearer of the two kinds' prices (plain 1, impedance
  `score.impedance`, pair `score.crossing_pair_net`), all times `score.crossing`. This is the pin study's rule
  (native/src/pinmap.rs:160-175) with the run score's pair-with-itself price kept. Confirmed by the user (D10). Cost if wrong: the
  pricing function in ratsnest.py, ratsnest.rs and pinmap.rs.
- R2 Measures carry a version, `"v": 2`. `report.comparable` treats a record without it as not comparable, so a stored
  best from the old definition reads as absent and the next run takes its place (as report.py:337-345 already does for
  pre-score records). Cost if wrong: one key.
- R3 The whole form of the incremental terms is computed from the occupancy, not the plan: `score.whole(board, occ)`.
  `plan_measures` calls it. The incremental-against-whole test needs the board's value at any moment of a resolve.
  Cost if wrong: a refactor of `plan_measures`.
- R4 The incremental change is taken against the lifted board: for a lifted item at a candidate,
  `whole(item at p) - whole(lifted)`. The search ranks candidates by it, refine and the turn check compare two of
  them. Lifting already refreshes the ratsnest and escapes (occupancy.py:1242-1251). Cost if wrong: none, the
  difference of two candidates is the same either way.
- R5 Give-way costs, `score.back_face` and `score.arrangement` stay where the scan adds them now (placer.py:332-377,
  layout.py:11063): their incremental form already is the action or the face the candidate takes. `unplaced`, `drc`
  and findings other than escapes are whole-board only (the spec's table). Cost if wrong: none, they are unchanged.
- R6 An item with a push or a declared escape lane keeps a Python delta (`score_delta.Delta` in Python), as
  `Scorer.native` returns None for it today (layout.py:889-890). Cost if wrong: speed on those items only (one on the
  core, refine spike).
- R7 The search's choice between clique and MST is a temporary choice setting, `place.search_wire` ("clique" or
  "mst", default "clique" until the gate), used only by the gate. Task 16 removes it either way: the clique code if the
  gate passes, the MST search path if it fails (refine and the turn check keep the MST delta). Cost if wrong: one
  setting for the length of the step.
- R8 `explore.congestion_step` becomes `score.congestion_step` (same default 0.05), and every run's measures carry
  `rudy_steps`. Congestion is a run-score term, so its step is a score setting. Migration entry. Cost if wrong: a
  rename.
- R9 The pin study's bend and group weights (`pins.bend_weight`, `pins.group_weight`) stay in `[pins]`: they have no
  run-score term. Its length weight becomes `1 / score.crossing` (today's 0.25 is that value at the default 4 mm), and
  its pair and impedance weights become D5's settings. `pins.pair_weight`, `pins.impedance_weight` and
  `pins.length_weight` are removed. Cost if wrong: three settings back.
- R10 The turn check reports a turn when `gain = change(here) - change(turned)` is at least `place.turn_gain_mm` and
  at least `place.turn_gain_share` of the item's own wire (its touched nets' airwire plus its links' term) at `here`.
  `place.turn_crossings_min` is removed: crossings are priced inside the same change. Cost if wrong: the threshold
  rule and one setting.
- R11 The reference runner gains `--place-only`: `placemat run` without `--route`, recording the placement measures
  (run score, crossings, airwire, area) under `results.json["placement"]`. Step 1 changes place_ref.py too (its Tasks
  5b, 5c, 17); this task touches only `main()`'s options and one new function, to keep the merge small. Cost if wrong:
  a merge conflict in one file.
- R13 Every incremental change is signed, and the search prunes only on proved lower bounds (spec amendment of
  2026-10-07, from a review of the plan). Stages, in order: (1) wire and crossings together, exact, from the MST
  change; (2) link, link_over, push, area, each exact and each never negative on the lifted board; (3) congestion,
  signed, lower bound `-score_congestion * steps(worst on the lifted board)`; (4) escapes, signed, lower bound minus
  the current prices of the pads whose airwire neighbours change. A candidate is pruned when the exact terms so far
  plus the lower bounds of the stages not yet run reach the best total. Cost if wrong: weaker pruning, never a wrong
  choice.
- R14 The exceptions to the one objective are named in the spec's "Exceptions" section (amendment of 2026-10-07): the
  pin study's bend and group terms; findings other than escapes judged only on the finished board; give-way,
  back-face and arrangement costs added by the scan; pushes and declared lanes in Python. The pin study keeps its own
  scorer to generate maps, and its finalists are rescored by the run-score change before any gain is reported or a
  map chosen (Task 13). Cost if wrong: one rescoring pass per study.
- R15 The speed gate is measured end to end as well as per candidate (outside review, item 7): Task 10 and the gate
  record, per item, lift and setup and commit time, the share of candidates scored in Python, legal candidates per
  second, and the resolve's wall time against the clique search on the same fixtures, beside the run score. A fast
  per-candidate score that slows the resolve or worsens the result fails the gate. Cost if wrong: a longer measure.
- R12 Order of building: Phase A (Tasks 1-6), B (7-10), C (11-16), then 17 after step 1. Phase A changes the run score
  without moving the search, so its re-recorded baseline measures the definition change alone (attribution). Cost if
  wrong: none.

In `ratsnest.py`, replace `_weight` and `_crossing_weight` with:

```python
KIND_PLAIN, KIND_IMPEDANCE, KIND_PAIR, KIND_PLANE = 0, 1, 2, 3     # pinmap_input.KINDS' order


def crossing_price(kinds, partners, prices, a, b) -> float:
    """What a crossing of nets `a` and `b` counts, in crossings (the run score multiplies by score.crossing): a quiet
    net's crossing is priced as a plane's; a differential pair's two halves crossing each other at the pair's own price;
    else the dearer of the two nets' kinds. `prices` is (plain, impedance, pair net, plane, a pair with itself). The
    pin study's rule (native/src/pinmap.rs `crossing`), with the run score's own price for a pair crossing itself."""
    ka, kb = kinds.get(a, KIND_PLAIN), kinds.get(b, KIND_PLAIN)
    if ka == KIND_PLANE or kb == KIND_PLANE:
        return prices[3]
    if partners and partners.get(a) == b:
        return prices[4]
    return max(prices[ka], prices[kb])
```

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Legality:
    critical: int = 0           # unplaced items plus critical findings (score.HARD_FINDINGS), from the measures
    drc_new: int | None = None  # DRC errors beyond the board's stripped or pre-route baseline; None when no DRC ran
    widths_failed: int = 0      # connections whose required width is not met on the final board (step 1's width
                                # judgement, joined_narrow included)


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Standing:
    measures: dict
    legality: Legality = Legality()
    closures: tuple = ()        # each routing phase's clean closure (0..1) in the phases' order, judged on the final
                                # board over the phase's fixed asked set; () when not routed
    clean: float | None = None  # the whole board's clean closure; None when not routed


def rank(a: Standing, b: Standing, fitted: bool, cfg) -> int:
    """-1 when `a` ranks above `b`, 1 below, 0 a tie. In order (D11):
    1. legality: (critical, drc_new, widths_failed) compared as a tuple, lower better; a drc_new of None counts 0

    5. the run score (the rest of it for a fitted module), with the score's noise band.

```

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `ratsnest.mst_change(rest_anchors, rest_edges, new_pads) -> (added_edges, dropped_edges, length_change)`: the
    MST of `rest_anchors + new_pads` is the MST of `rest_edges` plus every edge from a new pad to every anchor and to
    every other new pad (a theorem: the MST of a graph grown by vertices lies in the old MST plus the new vertices'
    edges). Kruskal on that edge set, KiCad's order key (`ratsnest.mst`, ratsnest.py:63-104). Returns the edges added
    and the rest edges dropped, and the length change.
  - `NativeDelta(rn, own, transforms, anchors, signal_nets, settings_tuple)` with
    `wire_cross(x, y, turn) -> (airwire_change, crossings_change)` in priced crossings.

The algorithm per candidate, for each net the item's pads touch that is not quiet:
1. `rest` is the net's anchors and MST edges as the lifted ratsnest holds them (lift removed the item's pads,
   occupancy.py:1242-1251).
2. Edges: rest edges, plus new pad to each rest anchor, plus new pad to new pad. Sort by (nm weight, ends' order key),
   Kruskal with a union-find over rest anchors plus new pads.
3. `airwire_change += k * (sum(len(added)) - sum(len(dropped)))`, `k` = `score_impedance` for an impedance net, else 1
   (D8).
4. Crossings: `crossings_change += price of added edges crossing every other net's edge (the grid, excluding dropped
   edges and edges of other touched nets, which step 5 handles) - price of dropped edges crossing the same`.
5. Among the touched nets: their new edge sets crossing each other, less their old edge sets crossing each other.

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Change:
    terms: dict          # term -> mm, the incremental terms of score.whole
    total: float


class Delta:
    """A lifted item's change in the run score at a candidate placement (R4), term by term: the native delta when the
    item has no push and no declared lane, else the Python twin (R6)."""
    def __init__(self, board, occ, item, links, pushes=(), lanes=None): ...
    def __call__(self, placement, prune_above: float = math.inf) -> Change: ...
    def native(self, rots, face): ...    # the object the native sweep calls, as Scorer.native hands it (layout.py:884)
```

Per term:
- `link`: `score_link * sum(w * |pad_a - pad_b|)` over declared links with an end on the item and the other placed;
  `link_over`: `score_link_over * w * max(0, length - limit)` on the same links.
- `push`: the item's pushes (`_push_value`, layout.py) and the pushes whose target is the item, at `score_push`.
- `area`: with a fitted frame, `score_area * (A(union(others' box, item box at p) grown by the margin) - A(others'
  box grown by the margin))`, on the fitted axes (Task 3 Step 6); `others' box` is the occupancy's placed content
  without the item (`Board._placed_box`, layout.py). Zero on a fixed outline.
- `congestion`: `RudyGrid` keeps per-cell demand of the placed nets as the lifted board has them (a touched net with
  its remaining pads) and a count of cells per congestion step. A candidate takes each touched net's lifted box out
  and puts its new box in, on a scratch diff of the cells and the step counts; the worst cell after is the highest step
  with a cell left. Signed: a grown box spreads a net's demand thinner and can lower the worst cell (R13). The change
  is `score_congestion * (steps(after) - steps(lifted))`.
- `escape_*`: Task 9.
- `airwire`, `crossings`: Task 7.
